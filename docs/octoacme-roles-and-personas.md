# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers lead the technical delivery side of a project by aligning engineering capacity, prioritization, and execution with the broader project plan. They serve as a bridge between Product/Project leadership and the engineering team.

### Responsibilities
- Support team planning, sprint health, and delivery forecasting
- Help resolve technical blockers and resource constraints
- Partner with Project Managers to align engineering commitments with milestones
- Ensure engineering quality, onboarding, and team capacity planning are accounted for
- Translate product needs into engineering execution plans and trade-offs

### Goals
- Improve delivery predictability and team effectiveness
- Reduce friction between product priorities and engineering execution
- Maintain a sustainable, high-performing engineering environment

### Typical Communication
- Weekly engineering planning and roadmap reviews
- Cross-functional delivery meetings with PM and Product roles
- Technical dependency updates and escalation communication

### Interaction with existing roles
- Works with Product Managers to prioritize and sequence technical work
- Coordinates with Project Managers on schedule, risk, and dependency management
- Supports Developers by removing obstacles and clarifying execution priorities
- Shares delivery status and risk signals with stakeholders as needed

---

## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
The Security / Compliance Lead ensures project work meets required standards for privacy, security, and governance. They help teams identify and mitigate risk before release or handoff.

### Responsibilities
- Review project work for security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Identify risk areas such as data handling, access control, and external dependencies
- Partner with engineering and PMs to include controls in planning and release gates
- Support incident response or security escalation when needed
- Maintain alignment with enterprise policy and relevant regulations

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk across the project lifecycle
- Ensure governance requirements are visible early and managed proactively
- Enable teams to release safely without avoidable blockers

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews and design discussions
- Risk assessments and compliance checkpoints
- Incident or escalation communications when necessary

### Interaction with existing roles
- Advises Product Managers and Project Managers on risk trade-offs and required controls
- Works with Developers to validate implementation choices and secure patterns
- Coordinates with stakeholders on compliance expectations and sign-off criteria
- Escalates high-risk issues to the relevant leadership or security channels when needed

---

## Data / Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
The Data / Analytics Lead helps the project track measurable outcomes, validate assumptions, and connect delivery work to business impact. They support evidence-based decision making throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define the metrics, dashboards, and reporting needed to measure success
- Support analysis of product performance, adoption, and quality signals
- Partner with Product Managers to validate whether outcomes are being achieved
- Work with QA and Developers to confirm data quality and instrumentation
- Translate operational data into actionable insights for the team and stakeholders

### Goals
- Make outcomes measurable and understandable
- Improve decision quality using evidence rather than assumptions
- Support continuous improvement through data-informed iteration

### Typical Communication
- Metric and KPI reviews
- Stakeholder reporting and dashboards
- Feedback loops for product, release, and operational metrics

### Interaction with existing roles
- Helps Product Managers define and monitor success metrics
- Works with Project Managers to report progress and risks from a performance perspective
- Collaborates with Developers and QA to confirm telemetry, test data, and quality signals
- Supports stakeholders with clear evidence for progress, trade-offs, and go/no-go decisions

---

## Customer Success / Support Liaison

### Role Summary
The Customer Success / Support Liaison connects delivery work to customer experience, support readiness, and operational continuity. They help ensure the team is building solutions that work in the real world and are sustainable after launch.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer feedback, pain points, and support trends
- Share post-release issues or adoption concerns with the project team
- Help define readiness checks for launch and support handoff
- Partner with Product and PM roles to ensure customer impact is considered in prioritization
- Identify training, documentation, and support needs required for rollout

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and adoption
- Reduce avoidable support issues and post-release disruption
- Ensure project outcomes are supported operationally after launch

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback channels and support issue reviews
- Launch readiness and stakeholder update meetings
- Escalation of service-impacting issues

### Interaction with existing roles
- Feeds customer insights to Product Managers for backlog prioritization and roadmap decisions
- Works with Project Managers to align release timing, support readiness, and stakeholder communications
- Partners with Developers and QA to validate real-world usage patterns and known issues
- Keeps stakeholders informed of customer impact, lessons learned, and follow-up needs

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles help clarify accountability across planning, delivery, quality, metrics, and customer impact.
